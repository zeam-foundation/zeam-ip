## Reading this from the outside

If you are checking whether "patent pending" is real, note how U.S. publication works before
concluding anything from a search that returns nothing:

- **Provisional applications are never published by the USPTO.** They confer a priority date and
  are not searchable. A provisional will not appear in Google Patents, ever.
- **Non-provisional applications publish at eighteen months from the earliest priority date.** The
  five utility applications below were filed 17 September 2025 and claim priority to provisionals
  filed from 23 May 2025, so they are scheduled to publish around **23 November 2026**.

Until then, absence from public patent search is the expected condition and is not evidence in
either direction. Application numbers can be verified by the applicant or an authorized party
through [USPTO Patent Center](https://patentcenter.uspto.gov/); the filings themselves are here.
